🧠 Built for Pure Flow & Direct Mental Dictation

When you type looking at the screen, your brain enters an endless supervisory loop: you generate an idea, type a word, and your visual cortex immediately evaluates it—triggering micro-pauses, premature editing, and broken trains of thought. TypeBuffer removes the visual monitor entirely while you write, letting you think and type in uninterrupted harmony.

The ergonomic & cognitive shift:

⚡ Bypassing the Visual "Supervisor"

Eliminate the urge to pause or micro-correct mid-sentence. Your brain routes thoughts directly from idea generation to motor execution, letting you write at the exact speed of thought.

🧘 Restorative Sensory & Visual Rest

Type with your eyes resting on a distant point or completely closed. Eliminate screen flicker, blue light, ocular muscle tension, and the dry-eye syndrome caused by staring without blinking.

🎯 The Expert Typist Advantage

For typists with honed muscle memory, blind typing transforms the keyboard into an effortless, friction-free mental dictation instrument—free from on-screen visual distraction.

🪄 The Intelligent Safety Net

Pause, and TypeBuffer cleanly flushes your accumulated sentence. If you made a minor typo or started with a language prefix (like en: or fr:), integrated AI instantly polishes or translates your text.

Why isn't blind typing taught to everyone? Traditional touch typing mandates on-screen monitoring because casual typists often commit accidental typos or subtle hand displacements off the home row, which without visual feedback can generate lines of unintelligible keystrokes. But for precision typists with high accuracy and reliable muscle memory, screenless typing shifts from a risk into an extraordinary cognitive superpower.

🪟 Ambient Zen Focus Overlay

An optional fullscreen typewriter canvas that awakens smoothly as you type and dissolves upon release. It dynamically adapts its background to your screen's ambient luminance so you write in pure, glare-free comfort.

Ambient Luminance Matched · WS_EX_NOACTIVATE Active

Ideas flow directly into the keyboard without visual evaluation

⚡ <30ms Hardware Luminance Samples the active display using Win32 GDI StretchBlt (0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B) to eliminate harsh visual glare.
🎯 Zero Focus Interruption Built with WS_EX_NOACTIVATE & WS_EX_TRANSPARENT. Your target editor, terminal, or browser never loses keyboard focus.
⌨️ Smart Tab & Flush Flow Enter and Ctrl+V release instantly. Pressing Tab as the first key suppresses the overlay, keeping field navigation uninterrupted.
🧘 Elegant Typewriter Aesthetics Centered 32pt monospace typography with 160ms fluid fade-in and fade-out transitions upon buffer release.

Engineered for Daily Frictionless Work

Reliable OS-level keyboard handling built for developers, writers, and multilingual professionals.

🪟 Zen Focus Overlay

An optional fullscreen typewriter canvas that fades in smoothly as you type. It dynamically samples your screen's average color and luminance to adapt contrast without eye strain, never stealing focus.

⚡ Instant Shortcut Passthrough

Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, arrow navigation, Esc and initial spaces pass through immediately without delay or buffering. Enter releases the buffer on the spot, so a chat message is typed out and sent in one go.

🎛️ System Tray & Settings

Hit Ctrl+Shift+Space or double-click the tray icon to pause and resume on the fly. Easily configure your preferred LLM provider, custom endpoints, and API keys.

🚀 Zero-Config Autostart

Configure TypeBuffer to run automatically on system login across Windows, macOS, and Linux without requiring admin rights.

🔒 Privacy-First Design

Your typing buffer is stored only in ephemeral memory. With translation and spellcheck disabled, no network packets ever leave your machine.
