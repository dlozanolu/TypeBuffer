🧠 Built for Pure Flow & Multilingual Speed

When you write looking at the screen, your brain enters a constant visual feedback loop: you think, you type, and you re-read every single character as it appears. For expert typists, this loop slows you down and breaks your train of thought.

TypeBuffer changes the paradigm:

⚡ Zero Visual Friction

Your keyboard inputs are stored silently in a high-speed buffer. No flickering cursors, no micro-corrections, no distractions.

🧘 Pure Kinesthetic Flow

Type with your eyes on a neutral point—or even closed. Transition directly from mental idea to muscle memory.

🪄 The Smart Buffer Dump

The moment you pause, TypeBuffer releases your sentence. If you typed in your language, it lands clean. If you used a prefix (like en: or fr:), AI transforms and injects it instantly in your target language.

The Core Pillars

Combining the physical focus of blind typing with the intelligence of modern LLMs.

🙈

Blind & Uninterrupted Typing

Write Without Looking

Eliminate visual fatigue and the temptation to edit mid-sentence. Type continuously into the buffer and let your full thoughts drop onto the screen in a single, satisfying batch.

🌐

On-The-Fly Translation

Think in Your Tongue, Output Anywhere

Simply prefix your typing (en:, fr:, es:). TypeBuffer translates your buffered text seamlessly before releasing it directly into Slack, Word, email, or your code editor.

✨

AI-Powered Proofreading

Flawless Final Output

Integrated with OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, and LanguageTool. TypeBuffer automatically cleans up grammar and typos in the buffer so your final text is always publication-ready.

🌐 Live Translation in Action

Type smoothly in your native tongue; TypeBuffer translates with near-zero latency and injects it straight into your active application.

You Type (in Spanish) en: Buenos días, ¿podemos revisar la reunión?
➔
App Receives (in English) Good morning, can we review the meeting?
You Type (in English) fr: Thank you very much for your help today.
➔
App Receives (in French) Merci beaucoup pour votre aide aujourd'hui.

Engineered for Daily Frictionless Work

Reliable OS-level keyboard handling built for developers, writers, and multilingual professionals.

⚡ Instant Shortcut Passthrough

Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, arrow navigation, Enter, Backspace, and initial spaces pass through immediately without delay or buffering.

🎛️ System Tray & Settings

Double-click the tray icon to pause or resume on the fly. Easily configure your preferred LLM provider, custom endpoints, and API keys.

🚀 Zero-Config Autostart

Configure TypeBuffer to run automatically on system login across Windows, macOS, and Linux without requiring admin rights.

🔒 Privacy-First Design

Your typing buffer is stored only in ephemeral memory. With translation and spellcheck disabled, no network packets ever leave your machine.

Getting Started

Run locally in seconds with Python or download a pre-built binary.
