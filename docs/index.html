How It Works

TypeBuffer intercepts your keyboard input silently. As you type, nothing shows up on your screen. Once you pause typing for 1.5 seconds (or instantly in translation mode), TypeBuffer runs your configured AI checker/translator and outputs the complete, polished sentence into your active window.

🌐 On-The-Fly Translation

Write in any language using simple prefixes (en:, es:, fr:, de:, etc.). The AI translates with zero extra delay directly into your chat or editor.

🔒 Ghost Typing

Hide your typing mistakes, hesitations, and backspaces. Focus your thoughts and let your final sentence appear clean and complete.

✨ Auto Proofreading

Integrated with LanguageTool, OpenAI, Claude, and DeepSeek. Automatically detects the language and corrects grammar with full sentence context.

⚡ Instant Passthrough

Shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V), arrows, Delete, Enter, and initial spaces pass through immediately without any lag or buffering.

🎛️ System Tray & Settings

Double-click the tray icon to pause or resume. Open the settings window anytime to tweak timeouts or add your favorite AI API keys.

🚀 Run on Startup

One-click autostart setup for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Runs unobtrusively in the background without needing administrator privileges.
